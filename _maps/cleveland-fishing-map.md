### Where to Fish Around Cleveland

These are spots in Cleveland Metroparks that the park district lists as major fishing areas, with the species it reports for each. Check the Metroparks fishing page and the Ohio regulations for current limits, and remember that anyone 16 or older needs an Ohio fishing license.

**Lakes and ponds**

- **Hinckley Lake** (Hinckley Reservation): an 87-acre lake, the largest inland lake in the Metroparks. It holds largemouth bass, channel catfish, crappie, bluegill and stocked rainbow trout. Metroparks calls it the best largemouth bass spot in the system. Electric trolling motors are allowed and the boathouse rents boats.
- **Wallace Lake** (Mill Stream Run Reservation, Berea): a 17.6-acre lake made from two flooded quarries. Rock piles, brushy shorelines and fallen trees hold largemouth bass, bluegill, crappie, catfish and carp.
- **Ledge Lake and Judge's Lake** (Hinckley Reservation): two small lakes, 4.5 and 2 acres, with bass, bluegill, crappie and stocked trout. Ledge Lake is shore fishing only.
- **Shadow Lake** (South Chagrin Reservation, Solon): 3.7 acres with largemouth bass, bluegill and channel catfish.
- **Ranger Lake** (Mill Stream Run Reservation, Strongsville): 1.8 acres with bass, bluegill, sunfish, crappie and stocked trout. The standing timber on the west side is worth casting around.
- **Beyer's Pond** (Big Creek Reservation): a 2-acre pond fished from shore, with largemouth bass, channel catfish, bluegill and crappie.
- **Rocky River Oxbow Lagoon** (Rocky River Reservation, North Olmsted): an 8-acre oxbow with brushy cover and deadfalls that hold largemouth bass, bluegill and crappie.

**Rivers and canal**

- **Rocky River**: steelhead runs in spring and fall, and smallmouth bass in summer. A stocked trout section on the East Branch runs through Mill Stream Run Reservation in March and April.
- **Chagrin River West Branch** (South Chagrin Reservation): smallmouth bass, steelhead and rock bass, best fished with light spinning gear or a fly rod. Stay on park property.
- **Cuyahoga River at the S.R. 82 dam** (Brecksville Reservation): carp, steelhead, smallmouth bass and catfish, reached from the towpath trail.
- **Ohio & Erie Canal** (Cuyahoga Heights): a managed one-mile stretch with bass, bluegill, crappie, catfish and trout.

Source: [Cleveland Metroparks fishing areas guide](https://www.clevelandmetroparks.com/getmedia/c7df5dfd-9c60-4cf3-8c40-dd6655cbdf67/CleveMetro_Fishing_areas.pdf).

### Gear for Fishing Around Akron

Akron's ponds, small lakes and rivers hold bass, panfish and catfish, so a simple setup covers most of them. These are the three things I'd start with. Once you have a rod, the [wacky rig guide](/5-tips-for-the-wacky-rig/) is an easy first technique for bass.
